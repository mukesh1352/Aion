# Ashoka and Kalinga

The story of Ashoka's transformation after the Kalinga War has become
one of the best-known narratives associated with his reign.

Historical evidence, especially Ashoka's inscriptions, records his
reaction to the suffering caused by the conflict.

Later Buddhist traditions developed more elaborate narratives around
Ashoka's life, conversion and relationship with Buddhism.

## What is historical?

Ashoka's own inscriptions provide direct historical evidence for his
reaction to the consequences of the Kalinga conflict.

## What belongs to later tradition?

Later literary and religious traditions contain additional narratives
about Ashoka that go beyond what can be established from his inscriptions.

AION keeps these layers distinct rather than presenting them as equally
certain historical events.
