# Classical India

Classical India saw the emergence and expansion of major
political powers, urban centres, philosophical traditions
and cultural networks.

The period includes the rise of the Mahajanapadas,
the Mauryan Empire, the spread of Buddhism and Jainism,
and later developments associated with the Gupta period.
