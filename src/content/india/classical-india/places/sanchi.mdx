# Sanchi

Sanchi is one of the most important surviving Buddhist archaeological
complexes in India.

The site developed over several centuries, with monuments and artistic
features added during different periods.

## Why it matters

Sanchi provides evidence for the development of Buddhist architecture,
art and religious practice across different historical periods.
