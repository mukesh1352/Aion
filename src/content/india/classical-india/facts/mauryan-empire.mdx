# The Mauryan Empire

The Mauryan Empire emerged in the late fourth century BCE and became
one of the largest political formations in ancient South Asia.

Its history is associated with rulers including Chandragupta Maurya,
Bindusara and Ashoka.

The empire connected numerous regions through political administration,
military power, trade and communication networks.
