# Satavahana Expansion in the Deccan

The Satavahanas developed a long-lived political presence across parts of the Deccan. They competed with Western Kshatrapas and participated in inland and maritime trade. Coins, inscriptions, caves and Buddhist monuments provide evidence for their political and economic networks.

This event belongs to a period of major political, religious, economic or intellectual change. The surrounding chronology matters because Classical India was not governed by a single continuous imperial structure.
