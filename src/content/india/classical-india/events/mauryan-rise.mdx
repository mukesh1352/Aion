# Rise of the Mauryan Empire

Chandragupta Maurya established a new dynasty around the beginning of the fourth century BCE. The process involved the defeat of the Nandas and expansion from the Magadhan core. Greek traditions preserve evidence for a powerful Indian ruler in the aftermath of Alexander's campaigns, while later Indian traditions connect the rise with Chanakya. The precise sequence of campaigns remains uncertain.

## Background

This event belongs to a period of major political, religious, economic or intellectual change. The surrounding chronology matters because Classical India was not governed by a single continuous imperial structure.

## What happened
