# Kalinga War

The Kalinga War was fought during the reign of Ashoka.

The conflict is particularly significant because of the way Ashoka's
later inscriptions describe the consequences of the war and his subsequent
approach to rule.

## Significance

The war is closely connected with the historical record of Ashoka and
forms an important part of the interpretation of his reign.
