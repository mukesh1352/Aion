# Kalinga War

Ashoka's Major Rock Edict XIII states that he conquered Kalinga and describes the deaths and deportations caused by the war. He presents the suffering as a source of remorse and as a reason for emphasizing dhamma. The exact battlefield locations and casualty figures cannot be independently reconstructed from archaeology.
