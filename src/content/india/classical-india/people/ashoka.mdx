# Ashoka

Ashoka was one of the most prominent rulers of the Mauryan Empire.

His reign is especially significant because of the large body of inscriptions
associated with him. These inscriptions provide important evidence for
understanding aspects of Mauryan administration, political ideas and
Ashoka's public policies.

## Why he matters

Ashoka's inscriptions are among the most important surviving sources
associated with a Mauryan ruler.
