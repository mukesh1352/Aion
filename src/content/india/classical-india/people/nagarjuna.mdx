# Nagarjuna

Nagarjuna was an influential Buddhist philosopher associated with the Madhyamaka tradition. He is generally placed somewhere in the second or third century CE, although the exact chronology remains disputed.

## Why they matter

Nagarjuna developed a rigorous analysis of dependent origination, emptiness, and the limits of fixed philosophical positions. His work became foundational for later Mahayana Buddhist philosophy in India, Tibet, China, Korea, and Japan.

## Historical evidence

The *Mulamadhyamakakarika* is the principal text associated with Nagarjuna and is the strongest basis for reconstructing his philosophical contribution. Later Indian, Chinese, and Tibetan sources preserve accounts of his life and influence.

The text tradition is much stronger evidence for his philosophical activity than the elaborate biographies that developed later.

## Traditions and later accounts

Later Buddhist biographies connect Nagarjuna with particular kings, monasteries, alchemical practices, and miraculous events. These accounts vary and often reflect sectarian or regional traditions.

The precise dates of Nagarjuna and the identification of all works attributed to him remain subjects of scholarly debate.
