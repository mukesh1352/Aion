# Chanakya

Chanakya is traditionally associated with Chandragupta Maurya and the
rise of the Mauryan Empire.

He is also associated with the *Arthashastra*, a major Sanskrit work
concerned with statecraft, economics, administration and political strategy.

## Tradition and history

The historical relationship between the figure remembered as Chanakya,
the authorship of the *Arthashastra*, and later literary traditions is
complex and debated.

AION therefore distinguishes the historical figure from later traditions
and interpretations.
