Iltutmish was one of the most important early rulers of the Delhi Sultanate.

His reign transformed a fragile political formation into a more stable state.

## From Slave Soldier to Sultan

Iltutmish had originally entered military service as a slave.

His abilities brought him into the political world of the Ghurid commanders in northern India.

He eventually became ruler of Delhi.

His career illustrates the unusual political mobility possible within the military elite of the early Sultanate.

## Consolidating Delhi

When Iltutmish came to power, his authority faced serious challenges.

Rival commanders, regional rulers and external threats all affected the stability of the sultanate.

Iltutmish gradually strengthened Delhi's authority over territories in northern India.

## Administration and Currency

Iltutmish is associated with important developments in the monetary and administrative structure of the Sultanate.

The silver tanka became an important currency denomination.

His government also developed systems for distributing and administering territories through military elites.

## Delhi as a Capital

Under Iltutmish, Delhi became increasingly important as the center of a major northern Indian state.

Architectural projects from the period contributed to the development of the city's early Sultanate landscape.

## Succession

Iltutmish is notable for recognizing his daughter Razia as a capable successor.

After his death, however, political struggles among competing elites shaped the succession.

Razia eventually became ruler.

## Historical Significance

Iltutmish was crucial to the survival of the early Delhi Sultanate.

His reign established political and institutional foundations that later sultans would expand and modify.
